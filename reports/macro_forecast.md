## Set-up

- **Series:** 15 monthly series from Eurostat (HICP, unemployment, industrial production, retail trade, bankruptcies), listed below. Values are the latest *revised* data, not real-time vintages, so all models share a mild look-ahead from revisions.
- **Protocol:** rolling origin, one forecast per month from 2016-01 (at least 48 months of history), horizons 1, 3, 12. Each forecast sees only data up to its origin. Classical specifications are re-selected every January on data up to that origin and re-estimated at every origin. The foundation models run zero-shot (no fine-tuning) on CPU.
- **Metrics:** MASE (scale: in-sample seasonal-naive MAE, m = 12, up to the origin), sMAPE, scaled pinball loss over the 10/50/90% quantiles (SPL, a CRPS approximation), 80% interval coverage. All models are scored on identical (series, origin, horizon) cells.
- **DM test:** Diebold–Mariano against seasonal naive on scaled absolute errors, (a) pooled (mean over series per origin), (b) per series (count of series significantly better / worse at 5%).

## Limitations

- Revised data, not real-time vintages; macro releases come with a lag the protocol ignores (the origin month's value is treated as known).
- One run per model: foundation-model sampling noise is not averaged (Chronos and TimesFM quantile heads are deterministic given the context, so this matters little).
- N-BEATS/NHITS were not run: `neuralforecast`/`darts` were not added, to keep the dependency set small. The global LightGBM is the trained-ML representative.
- `statsforecast` could not be used (requires pandas < 3); statsmodels ETS/ARIMA with a small selection grid stand in for AutoETS/AutoARIMA.
