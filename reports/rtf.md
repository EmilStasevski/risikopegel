## What this shows and what it does not

- It links the modules: Module A PDs drive credit capital and the normative path, Module B's VaR drives market capital, Module C's forecasts drive the macro scenarios (through the R4 satellite model), and the ECB curve drives IRRBB. Change an upstream model and the RTF figures change with it.
- It is not a bank's ICAAP. There is no liquidity, concentration, business or model risk; no behavioural deposit model beyond a fixed repricing profile; no tax; no management actions.
- `GET /rtf` on the API recomputes the economic perspective for other assumptions (LGD, book size, correlation scaling, confidence level).
